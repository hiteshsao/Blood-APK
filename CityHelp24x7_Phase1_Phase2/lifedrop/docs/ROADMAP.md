# City Help 24x7 — Product Roadmap

| Phase | Name | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blood Service | MVP peer-to-peer blood donation: SOS broadcast, donor matching, push notifications, privacy shield. |
| 2 | Service Providers | Hyperlocal service listings (plumbers, electricians, etc.) with booking and rating flows. |
| 3 | City Directory | Searchable directory of local businesses, government offices, hospitals, and emergency contacts. |
| 4 | News & Alerts | Hyperlocal news feed, civic alerts, weather warnings, and community announcements. |
| 5 | Jobs & Classifieds | Local job postings and classified advertisements for individuals and small businesses. |
| 6 | Buy & Sell | Peer-to-peer second-hand marketplace for buying and selling goods within the city. |


Phase 1 and Phase 2 are implemented. See `CITY_HELP_PHASES_1_2.md` for flags, API and QA steps.
